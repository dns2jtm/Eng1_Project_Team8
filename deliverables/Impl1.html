6(b) Assessment 1 features not (fully) implemented

All 17 Assessment 1 functional requirements in Req1.pdf are implemented and tested by 88 JUnit 5 tests (99.4% domain line coverage; traceability.html), plus four early A2 items (FR_CAMPUS_THEME, FR_GEESE_BLOCK, FR_GEESE_DISPERSE, FR_WIN_CONDITION).
