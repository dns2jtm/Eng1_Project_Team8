Suitability of licences (CR_LICENCES)

  • Commercial redistribution permitted: Because the customer may market and sell the game, all bundled components must permit commercial distribution. Permissive licences (Apache 2.0, BSD-2/3-Clause, zlib, MIT, SIL OFL 1.1) allow commercial sale provided copyright and licence notices are kept; GoosePatrol.jar includes THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt and full texts under licenses/. Apache 2.0 also grants patent rights.
  • LGPL v2.1 compliance in a single JAR: OpenAL Soft is extracted and loaded dynamically as a separate native library at run time, while JLayer and JOrbis remain unmodified Java packages inside a standard unobfuscated JAR (ZIP) archive. Under LGPL v2.1 Section 6, users can inspect and replace those classes with standard jar/zip tools; LGPL-2.1.txt and source links are included in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt.
  • Build/test tools and A2 asset rule: JUnit 5, JaCoCo, Gradle and PlantUML are not shipped in GoosePatrol.jar. Any Assessment 2 art or audio must be original work or licensed under CC0, CC BY or OFL (not GPL or CC BY-NC).
