Initial design (v1 – before implementation)

Design process evidence

Our architecture evolved through four traceable stages: (1) Week 3 noun/verb domain extraction from Req1 using Class–Responsibility–Collaborator (CRC) cards to isolate pure gameplay logic from libGDX OpenGL rendering; (2) Week 4 commitment of the baseline v1 PlantUML models (above, commit 91a6bc0); (3) Week 6 side-by-side architectural review reconciling two implementation spikes; and (4) synchronisation to the v2 models (top) verified against every requirement on traceability.html.

Stage 1: Initial domain CRC cards (Week 3)

GameWorld logic (Facade)

Responsibilities

  • Own session state (paused, running, won, lost)
  • Advance timer, entities, spawns & event checks per frame
  • Apply selected item to geese in range
  • Produce immutable end-of-game summary

Collaborators

  • Player
  • Goose
  • GameMap
  • HiddenEvent
  • GameTimer, GameStats
Entity / Player / Goose entity

Responsibilities

  • Maintain 2D position & collision radius
  • Slide smoothly along obstacle edges
  • Player: move on input; slow when mobbed; hold inventory
  • Goose: chase, eat, flee or disperse when fed

Collaborators

  • GameMap
  • Obstacle
  • Inventory
  • GooseState
GameMap & Obstacle entity

Responsibilities

  • Define campus world bounds & named locations
  • Test circle overlap against buildings & lake
  • Distinguish player walkability from goose water access

Collaborators

  • Obstacle (BUILDING, WATER)
Item & Inventory item

Responsibilities

  • Define item name, description & effect radius
  • Apply polymorphic effect (FoodItem, ScareItem) to a goose
  • Manage ordered item slots & active selection

Collaborators

  • Goose
  • Item subclasses
HiddenEvent event

Responsibilities

  • Remain invisible until player enters trigger radius
  • Guarantee at-most-once firing (Template Method)
  • Apply positive or negative world effect

Collaborators

  • Player
  • GameWorld
GameScreen, WorldRenderer & Hud screen

Responsibilities

  • Poll keyboard & forward commands to GameWorld
  • Render 2D campus, entities & triggered markers
  • Draw timer, counters, inventory & pause overlay

Collaborators

  • GoosePatrolGame
  • GameWorld (read/cmd)
  • libGDX GL/Batch

Stage 3: Week 6 side-by-side prototype comparison

Before finalising v2, we compared two working prototypes on a separate review branch: a compact 7-class flat-package build (commit 88ede63) and the layered 6-package architecture. The comparison below guided our final v2 structure:

CriterionFlat prototype (7 classes)Layered domain architecture (v2)Design decision
Automated testability Update & rendering coupled to libGDX runtime; 0 unit tests No GL/input imports in domain packages; 88 JUnit 5 tests (99.4% domain line coverage) Kept layered domain separation
Extensibility for A2 (QR_EXTENSIBILITY) Fixed 2 geese and hardcoded item/event fields Abstract Item and HiddenEvent hierarchies + centralised GameConfig Retained polymorphic hierarchies
Hidden event variety (FR_EVENT_COUNT) Introduced time-altering Slippery Path (−15 s) and Lucky Shortcut concepts Had Picnic Basket (+2 Bread) and Goose Nest (+3 geese) Ported both ideas into v2 as SlipperyPathEvent & LuckyShortcutEvent with zero edits to existing event classes
