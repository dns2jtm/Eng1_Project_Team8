Goose Patrol

A single-player libGDX game. You play a University of York student surrounded by hungry geese on campus. Use your items wisely and disperse the geese before the 5-minute timer runs out.

Play the game

Runnable JAR with all dependencies. Needs Java 21 or later.

How to play

KeyAction
P / ESCStart, pause and resume (the game starts paused)
W A S D / arrow keysMove
SPACEUse the selected item on all geese within range
1–9, Q / EChoose an item
R (end screen)Play again
